## Getting up and running

If you are new to Network Manager, follow these three steps to get your network operational as quickly as possible.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add your property">
    Register the building or site you manage, then add the individual units within it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add meters">
    Attach meters to the relevant units and configure the utility type each meter measures.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start billing">
    Record meter readings and generate bills to charge customers for their consumption.
  </Step>
</Steps>
