---

This guide covers common issues network managers encounter while working in Network Manager and provides steps to resolve them. If your issue is not listed here, contact Network Nation support at [hello@networknation.tech](mailto:hello@networknation.tech).

<Accordion title="A meter reading looks incorrect">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Compare the recorded value against the physical meter">
      Compare the value recorded in Network Manager against the reading on the physical meter to confirm whether there is a discrepancy.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Correct a manually entered reading">
      If the value was entered manually and is wrong, locate the reading in the Meters section and edit it to reflect the correct value.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check automatic meter connectivity">
      If readings are being acquired automatically, check the meter's status and connectivity. A meter shown as offline or flagged may not be transmitting accurate data.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Contact support if needed">
      If you cannot determine the source of the discrepancy or cannot correct it yourself, contact Network Nation support.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A customer's bill seems too high or too low">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Review the readings used in the bill">
      Open the bill in question and review the readings used to calculate it — check both the opening and closing values for the billing period.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm the correct meter is assigned">
      Confirm that the correct meter is assigned to the customer's unit. An incorrectly assigned meter will produce inaccurate bills.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check the billing period dates">
      Check the billing period dates to ensure the correct time range has been used.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Correct any erroneous reading">
      If a reading used in the bill appears incorrect, see the guidance above on correcting meter readings. Note that editing a reading after billing may require the invoice to be voided and re-issued — contact support if you are unsure.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A meter is showing as unassigned">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Locate the unassigned meter">
      Navigate to the Meters section and locate the unassigned meter.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Assign the meter to a unit">
      Assign it to the appropriate unit. If the unit does not yet exist, you must create it first in the relevant Property before completing the assignment.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm the assignment">
      Once assigned, confirm the meter appears correctly against the unit.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A customer is not receiving bills">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Verify the customer's contact details">
      Open the customer record and verify that their contact details — particularly their email address — are correct and up to date.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check the unit assignment">
      Check that the customer is assigned to a unit. A customer without a unit assignment cannot be billed.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm a meter is assigned to the unit">
      Confirm that the unit has at least one meter assigned to it.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check that bills have been generated">
      Verify that bills have been generated for the relevant billing period. If bill generation has not been run for that period, no bills will exist to send.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I can't find a customer record">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Search the Customers section">
      Use the search function in the Customers section to look up the customer by name or any other available identifier.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Check for deactivated records">
      Check whether the customer record may have been accidentally deleted or deactivated. Deactivated records may not appear in default list views.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Contact support for recovery">
      If you believe a record has been deleted in error, contact Network Nation support for assistance with recovery.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A property or unit does not appear in the list">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check the correct section">
      Confirm you are looking in the correct section — properties are listed under Properties, and units are found within their parent property.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Verify the record was saved correctly">
      Check whether the property or unit was saved correctly when it was created. An incomplete save may mean the record does not exist.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Refresh the page">
      Refresh the page, as the list may not have updated to reflect recent changes.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Contact support">
      If the record still does not appear, contact Network Nation support.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Bill generation is not producing bills for some units">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Check that every affected unit has a customer">
      Check that every affected unit has a customer assigned. Units without a customer cannot be billed.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Confirm meter assignments">
      Confirm that each unit has a meter assigned to it.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Verify there are at least two readings per meter">
      Verify that there are at least two readings recorded for each meter — a bill is calculated from the difference between two readings, so a single reading is not sufficient.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Review the billing period">
      Review the billing period selected during generation to ensure it covers the expected date range.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="A meter status shows as flagged or alerting">
  <Steps>
    <Step title="Review recent readings for anomalies">
      Open the meter record and review its recent readings for any anomalies, such as unexpectedly high or low values.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Correct any erroneous reading">
      If you identify an erroneous reading, correct it and check whether the status clears.
    </Step>
    <Step title="Contact support if the status persists">
      If the status persists after correcting the data, or if you are unable to identify the cause, contact Network Nation support for further investigation.
    </Step>
  </Steps>
</Accordion>

<Note>
  If your issue is not covered in this guide, the Network Nation support team is ready to help. Reach out at [hello@networknation.tech](mailto:hello@networknation.tech).
