You are here because the utility network you manage is moving onto **Network Manager**,
most likely from **FlexxView**. Nobody is going to run a training course for you — this
site is the training. It is written so you can find one answer, act on it, and get back
to work.

## Who this is for

You are a **network manager**: the person who runs a utility network day to day. You
check what residents owe, look into charges they query, record payments that arrived
outside the app, and keep resident records straight.

Your counterpart is the **resident** (the app calls them a *customer* or *end user*).
They use a completely separate application — different login, different app. Nothing you
do here is what they see.

## What to do on your first day

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get logged in">
    Network Manager requires two-factor authentication because of the personal data it
    holds. You will need an authenticator app on your phone. The QR code you are shown is
    used **once**, to set the authenticator up — you will not need it again, and you do
    not need to keep it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find your way to a balance">
    The first question almost everyone asks is *"what does this resident owe?"* Open
    **Accounts** in the sidebar. Learning this one screen answers a large share of
    everyday questions.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Learn the three words that sound alike">
    **Property unit**, **account** and **customer** are not interchangeable — a place, a
    bill payer, and a person. Mixing them up is the most common source of confusion in
    the first week. See the [Glossary](/reference/glossary).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find out what you can see">
    Your access is scoped, and a colleague may legitimately see more than you do. If a
    building is missing, that is usually configuration, not a fault.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Two things that surprise people moving from FlexxView

<Card title="Lists start grouped by property" icon="layer-group">
  **Accounts**, **Meters** and **Customers** all open grouped by property. The columns you
  are probably looking for — including **Balance** — only appear after you click
  **List all**. If a column seems to be missing, this is almost always why.
</Card>

<Card title="Some things genuinely are not here" icon="circle-minus">
  There is no data export, no meter tamper detection, and no alerting of any kind. These
  are not settings you have failed to find. Where the answer is "the platform cannot do
  this", this site says so plainly and tells you who to ask instead — that is more useful
  than letting you search for something that does not exist.
</Card>
