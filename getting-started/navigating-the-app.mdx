Network Manager is organised into sections that follow the core operational workflow. Each section corresponds to a stage in the process of managing your network, so you can move through them in order when setting up or jump directly to the area you need during day-to-day operations.

## Main sections

The table below describes each section and its purpose.

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Customers** | Add, view, and manage customer records. Each customer represents a person or organisation billed for utility consumption. |
| **Properties & Units** | Register the buildings and sites on your network, and manage the individual units — such as apartments — within each property. |
| **Meters** | Add and configure meters attached to units. Each meter is associated with a utility type and linked to a specific unit. |
| **Readings** | Record meter readings and review historical consumption data for any meter on your network. |
| **Billing** | Generate invoices based on recorded readings, track payment status, and manage disbursements. |

## Moving between sections

Each section is accessible from the main navigation. You can go directly to any section at any time — the app does not require you to follow a strict sequence during everyday use, though the workflow order is recommended when setting up a new property or customer for the first time.

<Tip>
  Use the search function to quickly locate a specific customer, property, unit, or meter without navigating through lists manually.
</Tip>

## Working across sections

Many tasks in Network Manager involve records from more than one section. For example, a meter is linked to both a unit (in Properties & Units) and a customer (in Customers). When viewing a record, related records from other sections are typically accessible directly from the same page, so you rarely need to leave your current context to find connected information.
