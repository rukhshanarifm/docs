Network Manager is a web application designed for network operators and managers who run private utility networks for apartment buildings and similar properties. It gives you a single place to manage every part of the billing cycle: registering customers and properties, tracking meter readings, and issuing invoices — across electricity, water, heat, and gas networks.

## Core workflow

Network Manager is built around a straightforward operational flow. Each step builds on the last, so working through them in order will get your network running smoothly from the start.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set up your customers">
    Add the individuals or organisations that will be billed for utility consumption on your network. Customer records are linked to the units they occupy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register properties and units">
    Create a property record for each building or site on your network, then add the individual units — such as apartments or commercial spaces — within each property.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add meters">
    Attach one or more meters to each unit. Each meter records consumption for a specific utility type: electricity, water, heat, or gas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record readings">
    Enter meter readings on a regular basis to capture consumption data. Readings form the basis for all billing calculations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate bills">
    Use the recorded readings to produce invoices for your customers. Network Manager calculates the charges and tracks payment status.
  </Step>
</Steps>
