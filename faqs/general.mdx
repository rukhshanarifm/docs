These are the most common questions from Network Manager users. If you don't find your answer here, the guides in the Help Center or the Network Nation support team can help.

<Accordion title="What utility types does Network Manager support?">
  Network Manager supports electricity, water, heat, and gas networks. You can manage meters and billing for any combination of these utility types within the same property.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I add a new building to my network?">
  Add the building as a Property in the Properties section. Once the property exists, you can add the individual apartments or spaces within it as Units. Meters and customers are then assigned at the unit level.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can a unit have more than one meter?">
  Yes. A unit can have one meter per utility type — for example, one electricity meter and one water meter. This allows you to track and bill multiple utilities independently for the same unit.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to billing history when I replace a meter?">
  The old meter's reading and billing history is preserved in Network Manager. The replacement meter starts a fresh reading history from the opening reading you record when it is installed. Both histories remain accessible for reference.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How are bills calculated?">
  Bills are calculated from the difference between the closing reading and the opening reading for each meter assigned to the customer's unit over the billing period. This consumption figure is then used to produce the invoice for that period.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I edit a reading after a bill has been generated?">
  You should contact Network Nation support before editing any reading that has already been used to generate a bill. Changing a billed reading may require the existing invoice to be voided and a corrected one to be issued. Reach out at [hello@networknation.tech](mailto:hello@networknation.tech) before making changes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I remove a customer who has moved out?">
  Update the customer's unit assignment to remove or end the tenancy. The customer's billing and reading history is retained in the system even after the assignment is ended, so you can still access past records when needed.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What should I do if automatic meter readings are not coming through?">
  Check the meter's status in the Meters section. If the meter shows as offline or flagged, this may indicate a connectivity or hardware issue. Contact Network Nation support at [hello@networknation.tech](mailto:hello@networknation.tech) for assistance with diagnosing and resolving the problem.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I view a customer's full billing history?">
  Yes. Open the customer's record and navigate to their billing history or invoices section. All bills generated for that customer are listed there, including details of the readings and periods they cover.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who do I contact for help?">
  For any questions or issues not covered in the Help Center, contact the Network Nation support team at [hello@networknation.tech](mailto:hello@networknation.tech). The team can assist with account queries, technical problems, and anything else you need.
</Accordion>
