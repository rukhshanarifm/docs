---

You enter a meter reading manually when a reading has not been automatically acquired — for example, after a field visit or when automatic data collection is unavailable. Manual entry ensures your billing data remains complete and accurate even when automation cannot capture a reading.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Readings section">
    Go to the Readings section of Network Manager, or locate the meter record directly from the Meters section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the meter">
    Find and select the meter you want to record a reading for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new reading">
    Click the option to add a new reading for the selected meter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the reading value and date/time">
    Enter the reading value as shown on the meter display, along with the date and time the reading was taken.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the reading">
    Save the reading to record it against the meter.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The reading value should be taken directly from the meter display. Double-check the value before saving.
</Note>

<Warning>
  An incorrect reading can result in an inaccurate bill. If you enter a reading in error, review and correct it before generating a bill.
</Warning>
