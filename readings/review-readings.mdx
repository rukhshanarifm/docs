---

Before generating bills, review your meter readings to ensure they are accurate and complete — catching errors at this stage prevents incorrect invoices from being sent to customers. Reviewing readings is a key step in the billing workflow.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Readings section">
    Go to the Readings section of Network Manager, or open the relevant meter record. Use the available filter options to narrow readings by meter or by date range, focusing on the period you are about to bill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Identify flagged or unusual readings">
    Look for readings that have been marked with alerts or flags. Also review any readings that appear unexpectedly high or low compared to previous values — these may indicate a data entry error or a metering issue that needs investigation before billing proceeds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Correct any errors">
    Select the reading you want to correct, edit the value, and save the change. Take care when editing readings — correcting a reading after a bill has already been generated may require re-issuing that bill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm readings are ready for billing">
    Once all readings for the billing period look correct and complete, you are ready to proceed to bill generation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Review readings at the end of each billing period, before running bill generation, to minimise corrections.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  If a reading has already been used to generate a bill, contact your Network Nation support contact at hello@networknation.tech before making changes.
</Warning>
