After generating bills, use the Billing section to view, send, and track invoices for each customer — keeping on top of invoice status helps you manage collections and identify unpaid accounts. Network Manager gives you the tools to handle the full invoice lifecycle from a single place.

## Viewing invoices

Navigate to the Billing section to see your invoices. Use the available filter options to narrow the list by customer, date range, or payment status to find the invoices you need quickly.

## Sending an invoice to a customer

Locate the invoice you want to send and select the option to send it to the customer. Make sure the customer's contact details are up to date before sending.

## Tracking payment status

Each invoice displays a payment status — such as paid, unpaid, or overdue. Regularly reviewing payment status helps you identify accounts that need follow-up and keeps your collections process on track.

## Handling a disputed or incorrect invoice

If a customer disputes an invoice, or if you identify an error, review the underlying readings first. Correcting an invoice may require voiding it and regenerating it from corrected readings.

<Warning>
  Voiding and reissuing an invoice affects your billing history. Contact Network Nation support at hello@networknation.tech if you need guidance on voiding and reissuing an invoice.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Payment records and billing history are retained even if a customer account is closed.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use the filter options to quickly find overdue invoices and follow up with customers.
</Tip>
