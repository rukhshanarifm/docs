---
title: "How to Generate Bills from Validated Meter Readings"
sidebarTitle: "Generate a Bill"
description: "Generate bills from validated meter readings once a billing period is complete — turn accurate, reviewed readings into invoices ready to send to customers."
---

Network Manager generates bills from validated meter readings — once your readings for a period are complete and reviewed, you can generate invoices for your customers. Taking the time to confirm your readings are accurate before this step avoids the need for corrections and re-issuance later.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirm readings are complete and reviewed">
    Ensure all meter readings for the billing period have been entered and reviewed for accuracy. Resolve any flagged or unusual readings before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Billing section">
    Go to the Billing section of Network Manager.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the billing period or customers to bill">
    Select the billing period, or choose the customers or units you want to generate bills for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initiate bill generation">
    Initiate the bill generation process. Network Manager will calculate charges based on the meter readings for the selected period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the generated bills">
    Review the generated bills before sending them to customers to confirm the amounts are as expected.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Bills are calculated from the difference between the current and previous reading for each meter.
</Note>

<Tip>
  If a customer has multiple utility meters (e.g. electricity and water), all meters are included in a single bill.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Make sure all readings are complete and accurate before generating bills. Generating a bill from incomplete data may require corrections and re-issuance.
</Warning>
