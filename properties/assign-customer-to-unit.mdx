---
title: "How to Assign a Customer to a Unit in Network Manager"
sidebarTitle: "Assign Customer"
description: "Assigning a customer to a unit links their account to a physical space, ensuring meter readings from that unit are billed to the correct customer."
---

Assigning a customer to a unit links their account to a specific dwelling so that meter readings recorded for that unit are billed to them. This connection between a customer and a physical space is what drives accurate billing across your network.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the property">
    Open the Properties section and select the property that contains the unit you want to assign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the unit">
    Locate the unit you want to assign a customer to within the property's unit list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the option to assign a customer">
    Choose the option to assign a customer to that unit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Search for and select the customer">
    Search for the customer by name or other identifying details, then select the correct customer record from the results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the assignment">
    Confirm the assignment. The customer will now be linked to the unit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A unit can only have one active customer assigned at a time. To move a customer to a different unit, first remove the existing assignment before creating a new one.
</Warning>

<Note>
  If the customer does not exist yet, add them first via the Customers section before returning to complete this assignment. See [Add a Customer](/customers/add-a-customer).
</Note>
