title: "How to Add and Register a Meter in Network Manager"
sidebarTitle: "Add a Meter"
description: "Register a new meter in Network Manager when a physical meter is installed on your network and you need to start recording its readings."
---

When a physical meter is installed on your network, you register it in Network Manager to start recording its readings and attributing consumption to the correct unit. Each meter must be added before readings can be logged or billing can begin.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Meters section">
    Open Network Manager and go to the Meters section from the main navigation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new meter">
    Select the option to add a new meter.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the meter details">
    Fill in the required fields, including the meter identifier or serial number, the utility type (electricity, water, heat, or gas), and any other required fields presented in the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the meter record">
    Confirm and save the meter record. The meter will now appear in your Meters list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  After adding the meter, assign it to a unit so readings can be attributed to a customer. See [Assign a Meter to a Unit](/meters/assign-meter-to-unit).
</Note>

<Tip>
  The meter's serial number or identifier should match the label on the physical device to avoid mismatches between field records and the system.
</Tip>
