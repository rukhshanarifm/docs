---
title: "How to Assign a Meter to a Unit in Network Manager"
sidebarTitle: "Assign Meter"
description: "Link a meter to a unit within a property so that the meter's readings are attributed to the correct customer for accurate billing."
---

Assigning a meter to a unit connects the meter's readings to the customer in that unit, enabling accurate billing. Until a meter is assigned, its readings cannot be attributed to any customer or dwelling.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Meters section or the relevant unit">
    Open Network Manager and go to the Meters section, or navigate to the relevant unit within the Properties section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the meter">
    Find and select the meter you want to assign.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the assign option">
    Select the option to assign the meter to a unit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the property and unit">
    Choose the property and the specific unit within that property that the meter should be assigned to.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the assignment">
    Review the details and confirm the assignment. The meter is now linked to the selected unit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Each unit can have one meter per utility type. Assigning a second electricity meter to the same unit will replace the existing assignment. Make sure you intend to replace the current assignment before proceeding.
</Warning>

<Note>
  The unit must already exist in the system before you can assign a meter to it. If the unit doesn't exist yet, create it first in the Properties section.
</Note>
