Network Manager displays the status of each meter so you can quickly identify devices that need attention, such as meters that are offline or have flagged readings. Reviewing statuses regularly helps you catch and resolve issues before they affect billing or reporting.

## Meter statuses

<Accordion title="Active">
  The meter is operating normally and readings are being recorded. No action is required for meters in this status.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Inactive / Decommissioned">
  The meter has been removed from service or is no longer in use. This status is typically set when a physical meter is replaced or permanently taken offline. Historical data for the meter is preserved and remains accessible.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Alert / Flagged">
  The meter has a reading or data issue that requires your review. This may indicate an unusually high or low reading, a missing reading, or a data transmission problem. You should review the flagged data and take corrective action.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Unassigned">
  The meter has been added to Network Manager but has not yet been assigned to a unit. Readings from an unassigned meter cannot be attributed to a customer. Assign the meter to a unit to enable billing attribution.
</Accordion>

## Responding to alerts

When a meter shows an Alert or Flagged status, take the following steps:

1. Open the meter's record to review the flagged reading or data issue.
2. Determine whether the reading is incorrect, missing, or anomalous.
3. Correct the reading if you have the accurate data available.
4. If the issue is unclear or cannot be resolved from within the system, contact your Network Nation support contact for assistance.

Resolving alerts promptly prevents data issues from compounding and reduces the risk of billing errors.

<Tip>
  Review meter statuses regularly to catch data issues before they affect billing. Consider building a routine check into your weekly or monthly workflow.
</Tip>
