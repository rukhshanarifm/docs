---
title: "How to Add a New Customer Record in Network Manager"
sidebarTitle: "Add a Customer"
description: "Learn how to create a new customer record in Network Manager when onboarding a new resident or account holder to your utility network."
---

When you onboard a new resident or account holder to your network, you need to create a customer record in Network Manager. This record holds the customer's contact information and serves as the basis for linking them to a unit and generating bills.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Customers section">
    Open Network Manager and select **Customers** from the main navigation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a new customer">
    Click the button to add a new customer. A form will open for you to enter the customer's details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the customer's details">
    Fill in the customer's name and contact information, such as their email address and phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the customer record">
    Review the details you have entered, then save the record. The new customer will appear in your customer list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The customer must be assigned to a unit before billing can begin. Creating a customer record alone is not enough to generate invoices.
</Note>
