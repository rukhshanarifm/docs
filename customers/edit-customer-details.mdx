---
title: "How to Update Customer Contact Details in Network Manager"
sidebarTitle: "Edit Customer"
description: "Learn how to update an existing customer's name and contact information in Network Manager to keep billing and communication records accurate."
---

When a customer's contact information changes — for example, they move to a new email address or update their phone number — you need to update their record in Network Manager. Keeping customer details current ensures that billing communications reach the right person.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Customers section">
    Open Network Manager and select **Customers** from the main navigation menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find and select the customer record">
    Locate the customer you want to update. You can scroll through the list or use the search feature to find them by name or contact details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the edit option">
    Select the option to edit the customer record. The customer's details will become editable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update the relevant fields">
    Change the fields that need updating, such as the customer's name, email address, or phone number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your changes">
    Review the updated information, then save the record. The changes will take effect immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Changing a customer's contact details may affect billing communications. Ensure the new information is correct before saving, as invoices and notifications will be sent to the updated contact details.
</Warning>
