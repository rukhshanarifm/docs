This guide covers the day-to-day account management tasks you will carry out in the Customers section of Network Manager. You will learn how to view a customer's account summary, check their billing history, and deactivate or remove a customer when they leave the network.

## Viewing a customer's account summary

The account summary gives you an at-a-glance overview of a customer's current status, their linked unit, and any outstanding activity on their account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Customers section">
    Navigate to **Customers** in the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the customer">
    Select the customer whose account you want to review.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the account summary">
    The account summary will display on the customer's profile page. Use it to confirm which unit a customer is assigned to and whether there are any items that require your attention.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checking a customer's billing history

Billing history shows all invoices that have been generated for a customer, along with their status.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer's profile">
    Open the customer's profile from the **Customers** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to billing history">
    Navigate to the billing history section of the profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review invoices">
    Review the list of invoices. You can select an individual invoice to see its full details.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Use the billing history to resolve disputes or answer customer queries about past charges.
</Tip>

## Deactivating or removing a customer

When a customer leaves the network — for example, when a resident moves out — you should deactivate or remove their account to stop further billing.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the customer's profile">
    Open the customer's profile from the **Customers** list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the deactivate or remove option">
    Select the option to deactivate or remove the customer.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the action">
    Confirm the action when prompted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Removing a customer does not delete their billing history. All past invoices and account records are retained and remain accessible for reference and auditing purposes.
</Note>
